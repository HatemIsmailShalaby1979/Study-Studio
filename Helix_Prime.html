Local AI learning

Lessons, quizzes, glossaries, and podcasts from your study material.

Study Studio is local-first and multi-provider: it runs through Ollama, LM Studio, or any OpenAI-compatible local server, with optional online providers, and Piper TTS for speech. It is the learning engine behind Helix Prime and Helix Education. Latest release: v0.1.0 for Windows x64; v0.2.0 is in development.

Features

Built around local generation

Lessons and quizzes

Turn a topic or study material into structured learning content.

Podcasts and voices

Generate podcasts with four real voices, including Arabic (Jordanian).

On-demand export

Create MP3 or WAV audio files when requested.

Multi-provider runtime

Auto-detects local runtimes (Ollama, LM Studio, LocalAI, vLLM, LiteLLM, FastChat) and optional online providers (OpenAI, OpenRouter), with a live provider selector on the Generate page.

Current status

v0.2.0 in development · v0.1.0 released

v0.1.0 ships NSIS, MSI, and portable installers for Windows x64 and requires a local AI runtime plus at least one chat model. v0.2.0 adds the multi-provider AI Runtime and a CORS-free desktop transport, so any local server works regardless of its CORS configuration. Piper TTS and ffmpeg are optional for audio export; MP3 export requires ffmpeg.

The Helix ecosystem

Learning engine for Helix Prime and Helix Education

Study Studio is a standalone, independently versioned repository that powers the Helix platform: Helix Prime uses it as its AI lesson, quiz, and podcast generation engine, and Helix Education consumes its quiz and evaluation event stream. Study Studio stays separate so it can be versioned and released on its own.
